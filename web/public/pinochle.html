Partnership Auction Pinochle

Teams: you and your partner across the table against the two players beside you. 48-card deck (two each of A, 10, K, Q, J, 9 per suit), 12 cards each. Ranking: A > 10 > K > Q > J > 9.

Bidding: starts left of the dealer at 100, in steps of 10. Once you pass you're out. The last bidder names trump and leads first. If everyone else passes without a bid, the last player is stuck with 100.

Meld: run in trump (A-10-K-Q-J) 150 · royal marriage (K-Q of trump) 40 · marriage 20 · pinochle (J♦ Q♠) 40 · double pinochle 300 · dix (9 of trump) 10 · aces/kings/queens/jacks around 100/80/60/40. Meld counts only if your team wins at least one trick.

Play: follow suit and beat the winning card if you can. If you can't follow, trump (and overtrump if possible). Otherwise play anything. Equal cards: the first one played wins.

Scoring: aces, tens and kings are 10 each, and the last trick is 10. The bidding team scores meld + counters if it reaches the bid, otherwise it loses the bid. The other team scores what it took. First team to 1,000 wins (the bidding team wins ties).
